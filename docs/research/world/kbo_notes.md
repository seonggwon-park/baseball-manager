## a. KBO regular-season format

**Size and opponents**
- Single league, 10 clubs. Each club plays 144 games, 16 against each of the 9 opponents; 720 games in total. Source: REG Article 1 (https://6ptotvmi5753.edge.naverncp.com/KBO_FILE/ebook/pdf/2026_리그규정.pdf); 2026 schedule release https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11794

**Home/away split**
- Not 72/72. Under the KBO's alternate-year principle, five clubs get 73 home / 71 away and the other five 71 / 73, swapping every year. 2026: LG, Samsung, NC, KIA, Kiwoom had 73 home games; KT, SSG, Doosan, Lotte, Hanwha had 71. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11794 ; confirmed by counting home games in ATT (https://www.koreabaseball.com/Record/Crowd/GraphDaily.aspx).
- 2025 was the reverse (KT, SSG, Doosan, Lotte, Hanwha 73). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=10218
- By that principle the 2027 73-home clubs should be KT, SSG, Doosan, Lotte, Hanwha, but the 2027 schedule is not published yet (expectation, not an announcement).

**Series pattern (2026)**
- Opening weekend is a 2-game series (Sat-Sun). The first series after the All-Star break is a 4-game series (Thu-Sun). Every other scheduled series is a 3-game series. There are no other 2-game series. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11794
- 135 games per club were scheduled this way through Sunday 6 September. The other 9 games per club (45 games league-wide) were left unscheduled and placed later, together with rained-out games, in a "remaining games" schedule. Source: same release.
- Typical week: Tuesday-Thursday series, Friday-Sunday series, Monday off. Actual 2026 games by weekday: Tue 128, Wed 119, Thu 113, Fri 108, Sat 124, Sun 120, Mon 8 (the Monday games were make-ups on 5 and 12 October). Source: computed from ATT (https://www.koreabaseball.com/Record/Crowd/GraphDaily.aspx).

**Start times (REG Article 21)**
- Weekdays 18:30 all season.
- March-May: Saturday 17:00, Sunday/holiday 14:00. June: Saturday and Sunday 17:00. July-August: Saturday and Sunday 18:00. 1-14 September: Saturday and Sunday 17:00. From 15 September and in October: Saturday 17:00, Sunday/holiday 14:00.
- Opening 2-game series and Children's Day: 14:00. Gocheok dome Sunday games in June-August: 14:00.
- Source: REG Article 21 (https://6ptotvmi5753.edge.naverncp.com/KBO_FILE/ebook/pdf/2026_리그규정.pdf)

**Extra innings, ties, winning percentage**
- Regular season: extra innings up to the 11th inning (reduced from 12 in 2025); still level after 11 = tie. Postseason: up to 15 innings. No tie-break runner in the first-team league. Source: REG Article 1.2; board decision https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11353
- Winning percentage = wins / (wins + losses). Ties are left out of the calculation. Source: REG Article 2.
- Standings ties (REG Article 3): a two-way tie for 1st or for 5th is settled by a one-game tiebreaker the day before the wild-card series (home club = better head-to-head record, then head-to-head runs, then previous year's rank). Three or more clubs tied for 1st/5th, and any tie for 2nd-4th: head-to-head record among the tied clubs, then head-to-head runs scored, then previous year's rank. Ties for 6th-10th share the place.

**Doubleheaders and rain-out make-ups (2026)**
- A doubleheader could be scheduled only between Sunday 12 April and Sunday 31 May, only on a Sunday, and only when that club's Saturday game was cancelled. A club cannot have doubleheaders in two consecutive weeks. Both games are limited to 9 innings (no extra innings). On a doubleheader day a club may add 2 players to its entry ("special entry"). Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11878 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11879
- Other cancelled games are not replayed the next day; they go into the remaining-games schedule. REG Article 5 lets the commissioner use Monday games and doubleheaders for postponed games.
- Remaining-games schedule (published 25 August: 45 unscheduled + 60 postponed = 105 games, through 7 October): a cancelled game goes first to a listed reserve day; with no reserve day, to a doubleheader on the second day of the next series between the same clubs, or is scheduled later. From 29 September a next-day doubleheader is used when the same clubs meet the next day. No doubleheaders during the Asian Games; doubleheaders possible from 29 September, at most one per club between 29 September and 5 October. Doubleheader start times 15:00 and 18:30 on weekdays, 14:00 and 17:00 on weekends/holidays; game 2 starts at least 40 minutes after game 1 ends. Make-ups may create runs of up to 9 consecutive game days. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12120
- A second remaining schedule (11 games, through Monday 12 October) was issued on 30 September. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12172
- No doubleheader appears in the 2026 ATT data (no date has the same pairing twice). Source: computed from ATT.

**Key dates, 2026 season**
- Non-activity period and spring camp: since the 2025 season the non-activity period is 24 November - 24 January and clubs may start spring camp on 25 January (clubs may shift start/end by up to 7 days). Source: board decision of 31 July 2024, https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=10160 . The October 2026 board release states that all clubs have started on 25 January since 2025: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12184 . (The printed 2026 bylaws still show the older text: salary period 1 February - 30 November in Article 69 and no team training 1 December - 31 January in Article 144.)
- Exhibition games (시범경기): 12-24 March 2026, 12 games per club (60 total), all at 13:00, no extra innings, no doubleheaders, cancelled games not replayed, no limit on players used (registered and developmental players eligible). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11823
- Opening Day: Saturday 28 March 2026 (later than usual because of the March 2026 World Baseball Classic); openers hosted by the top five clubs of 2025. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11794 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11687
- All-Star Game: Saturday 11 July 2026 at Jamsil (Futures All-Star Game and home run derby on Friday 10 July). All-Star break 10-15 July (6 days); second half began Thursday 16 July. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11794 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11898
- Heat-wave stoppage (unplanned): all KBO and Futures games on 5-6 August were cancelled and the league resumed on Tuesday 11 August, with 19:00 starts until late August and fixed cooling breaks. ATT shows no games between 5 and 10 August. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12095 , https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12097 , https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12108
- No break for the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games (September); instead the entry was enlarged (section d). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11980
- End of the regular season: last games played Monday 12 October 2026 (first games 28 March). Source: ATT (https://www.koreabaseball.com/Record/Crowd/GraphDaily.aspx)
- Postseason: wild card from Wednesday 14 October; Korean Series 3-8 November (Samsung beat regular-season champion KT 4-1). Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12183 , https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12221 , result: https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1172862
- Bylaw frame: the regular season is to be held between 20 March and 31 October (BYLAW Article 129); the full schedule is published by the end of December of the previous year (Article 130).

**Key dates, 2027 season**
- Announced: the contract "participation period" becomes 25 January - 23 November and the non-activity period 24 November - 24 January, in force from 1 January 2027; spring camp therefore starts 25 January 2027. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12184
- NOT ANNOUNCED as of 2026-11-16: exhibition dates, Opening Day, All-Star Game date and venue, end of season. No KBO release up to release no. 12242 (13 November 2026) gives them; the 2026 schedule was published on 19 December 2025. Checked: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/List.aspx
- The KBO said it will consider measures for the peak-heat period when drawing up future schedules (no decision). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12097

## b. Postseason format (2026; REG Chapters 2-5)

Sources for this section: REG Articles 30-46 (https://6ptotvmi5753.edge.naverncp.com/KBO_FILE/ebook/pdf/2026_리그규정.pdf) and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12183

- Five clubs qualify (places 1-5 by regular-season winning percentage).
- Wild-card series (4th vs 5th): at most 2 games, both at the 4th-place club's park. The 4th-place club advances with one win or one tie; the 5th-place club must win both games.
- Semi-playoff (3rd vs wild-card winner): best of 5. Games 1, 2, 5 at the 3rd-place club; games 3, 4 at the wild-card winner.
- Playoff (2nd vs semi-playoff winner): best of 5. Games 1, 2, 5 at the 2nd-place club; games 3, 4 at the semi-playoff winner.
- Korean Series (regular-season champion vs playoff winner): best of 7 in a 2-3-2 layout since 2025. Games 1, 2, 6, 7 at the regular-season champion; games 3, 4, 5 at the playoff winner. (Before 2025 it was 2-2-3.) Source for the change: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11353
- At least one travel day between series.
- Extra innings up to the 15th; then a tie. A tied game (other than in the wild card) is replayed after the scheduled last game of the series, at the park where the tie happened.
- Since 2025 there are no rain-shortened or "no game" results in the postseason; an interrupted game is suspended and resumed from the point of interruption, one game per day. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12206
- Postseason entry: 30 players registered, 28 usable per game; 4 foreign players registered and usable (3 + the Asia quota player). Eligibility: on the club's roster by 31 July (foreign players registered by 15 August; discharged military players by the end of the season). Source: REG Article 45.
- Video review: 2 per club in regulation (+1 if both succeed, +1 in extra innings); check-swing review as in the regular season.
- Final ranking: Korean Series winner 1st, runner-up 2nd, the rest by regular-season winning percentage (REG Article 3).

## c. Futures League format in 2026

- 12 teams (11 in 2025). New: Ulsan Whales, a citizen club run by Ulsan city, admitted to the Southern League; Sangmu (military) moved from South to North, giving 6 + 6. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11788 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11841
  - 북부리그 (North): Sangmu, Hanwha, LG, SSG, Doosan, Goyang (Kiwoom's farm team).
  - 남부리그 (South): KT, NC, Lotte, Samsung, KIA, Ulsan.
  - Source: REG Article 58.
- Games: 121 per team (116 in 2025): 17 against each of the 5 teams in the same league (85) + 6 against each of the 6 teams in the other league (36; 3 home, 3 away). 726 games in total. Sources: REG Article 59; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11814
- Dates: opened Friday 20 March 2026, regular season ended Sunday 20 September 2026. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12159
- Weekly pattern (new in 2026): series Wednesday-Friday and Saturday-Monday; Tuesday is the fixed off day (first-team league is off on Monday). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11814
- Start time 13:00. "Summer league" 15 July - 24 August: all games at 18:00. Ulsan weekday home games 18:30. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11841
- Tie rule: tie-break innings (runners on 1st and 2nd, batting order continues) from the 10th to the 12th inning, then a tie (until 2025 it was played to a decision). Source: REG Article 59.2.
- Rain-outs: made up as a next-day doubleheader only in April and May; both games 7 innings, no tie-break; entry 28 instead of 26 on doubleheader days. Other cancelled games are generally not replayed. Sources: REG Article 60; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11841
- Consequence: teams actually played only 94-104 of the 121 scheduled games in 2026 (rain, ground conditions, and the August heat stoppage, when all games through 10 August were cancelled). Sources: final standings https://www.koreabaseball.com/Futures/TeamRank/North.aspx and https://www.koreabaseball.com/Futures/TeamRank/South.aspx ; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12097
- Futures active entry: 26 players. A player removed from the first-team entry can be registered for Futures games at once. Source: REG Article 68.
- Championship (second edition; introduced in 2025): the top two of each league qualify. Semifinals are single games at the home of each league winner: North 1st vs South 2nd, South 1st vs North 2nd. The final is a single game at Gocheok Sky Dome, home team = finalist with the better regular-season winning percentage. In championship games the tie-break is played until decided, video review is used, and only suspension applies in rain. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12152
  - 2026: semifinals 22 September (Sangmu 5-2 Lotte; Ulsan 2-0 Hanwha); final 28 September, Sangmu 8-3 Ulsan. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12167 ; final score: https://v.daum.net/v/1lDbucu5U3?f=p
- 2026 final standings (W-L-T): North: Sangmu 62-34-0, Hanwha 55-46-0, LG 50-51-1, Doosan 45-59-0, Goyang 43-59-0, SSG 41-60-0. South: Ulsan 58-37-1, Lotte 54-39-1, NC 58-44-0, KT 46-52-1, KIA 43-55-0, Samsung 42-61-0. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/Futures/TeamRank/North.aspx and https://www.koreabaseball.com/Futures/TeamRank/South.aspx
- Home parks used in 2026 (KBO schedule labels): 문경 (Sangmu), 서산 (Hanwha), 이천(LG), 이천(두산), 고양, 강화 (SSG), 익산 (KT), 마산 (NC), 상동 (Lotte), 경산 (Samsung), 함평 (KIA), 울산 (Ulsan). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/Futures/Schedule/GameList.aspx
- Rule differences from the first-team league are listed in section e.
- "Futures League participating club" status (Ulsan): REG Article 78 (new, 19 November 2025). No draft rights; own contract form; at least 7 coaches and 35 players to be filed by 15 January each year; up to 5 players per season may move to KBO clubs (transfer fee capped at the player's salary); foreign players max 4 at US$100,000 each at admission. The executive committee raised the foreign-player limit for such clubs to 6 on 2 June 2026 (of the 5 transfers per season, at most 4 may be foreign players). Sources: REG Article 78; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11980

## d. Roster-size rules (2026)

**Registered players (소속선수 정원)**
- Maximum 68 per club including reserved players, raised from 65 in January 2026. Players doing military service are not counted. Sources: BYLAW Article 24 (https://6ptotvmi5753.edge.naverncp.com/KBO_FILE/ebook/pdf/2026_야구규약.pdf); https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11814
- Reserve list (보류선수) for the next season: at most 63 (military and developmental reserved players not counted). Source: BYLAW Article 57.

**First-team entry (현역선수 등록)**
- 2026 base rule: 29 registered, 27 may be used in a game (the two unavailable players are designated before each game). Up from 28 / 26, because of the Asia quota. Sources: REG Article 14.1 and 14.6; board decision https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11353
  - Conflict noted: the KBO web page PAGE says "28 registered, 27 used -> 29 registered, 28 used". The printed regulations (29 / 27) and the board release (28/26 -> 29/27) agree with each other, so the web page is treated as a typo. https://www.koreabaseball.com/Kbo/League/GameManage2026.aspx
- 2026 second half only (after the All-Star break), because clubs sent players to the Asian Games: 30 registered, 28 used. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11980
- Expanded entry: printed rule = from 1 September, +5 (34 registered, 32 used). Changed on 2 June 2026: expansion starts on 25 August, and in 2026 the addition was +4 on top of 30, so still 34 registered / 32 used. Sources: REG Article 14.1; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11980
- Doubleheader days: +2 players for that day. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11879
- Postseason: 30 registered, 28 used. Source: REG Article 45.
- Registration and removal must be filed 2 hours before the game. Source: REG Article 14.6.

**Minimum time after being sent down**
- A player removed from the first-team entry cannot be re-registered until 10 days have passed from the announcement of the removal. Exceptions: bereavement/paternity/reserve-forces leave (up to 5 days, counted as service time), a player traded to another club, and the injured-list extension case below. Sources: BYLAW Article 27; REG Article 14.2; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11878

**Injured list (부상자 명단)**
- For a first-team player injured in a regular-season game or practice; from 2026 also for a registered player injured in a game or practice after the opening day of the exhibition season who misses the opening entry (application within 3 days of the opening-entry announcement).
- Terms of 10, 15 or 30 days; at most 30 days in total per player per season (three 10-day terms, or two 15-day terms, or one 30-day term). Application with a diagnosis within 3 days of removal from the entry.
- The player is off the entry but the days count as first-team service time. He cannot be re-registered until 10 days after going on the list; from an extension for the same injury (second application onward) he may return before 10 days.
- The removal can be deferred for up to 3 days after the player's last game while the injury is assessed.
- If a club forgot to apply for a player who was off the entry for 30+ days with an obvious injury, service time can be credited retroactively, at most 3 cases per club per year.
- Separate "treatment/rehab list" (치료·재활선수 명단): minimum 10 days, for any registered or developmental player, no service-time credit.
- Sources: REG Article 14.3-14.4; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11795

**Foreign players**
- A club may contract at most 3 foreign players, and all 3 may not be registered at the same position (so not 3 pitchers or 3 hitters). Source: 외국인선수 고용규정 Article 3 in BYLAW.
- In one game up to 3 may play; with the Asia quota player, up to 4. So all four may appear in the same game. Source: 외국인선수 고용규정 Article 4; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11879
- Replacements: 2 additional registrations per season for regular foreign players, 1 for the Asia quota player. A foreign player registered after 15 August cannot play in the postseason. Source: 외국인선수 고용규정 Article 9.
- Injury-replacement foreign player: allowed when a foreign player is diagnosed out for 6 weeks or more; the injured player cannot return for at least 6 weeks; once per player. Source: 외국인선수 고용규정 Article 10.

**Asia quota player (아시아쿼터), new in 2026**
- One extra player per club, any position, in addition to the 3 foreign players.
- Eligible: nationals of Asian Baseball Federation (BFA) member countries and Australia; dual nationals holding a non-Asian nationality are not eligible; must have belonged to an Asian league in the previous or current year.
- Counts as a 4th foreign player for registration and game use; the first-team entry grew by one because of it.
- Cost cap for a new signing: US$200,000 in total (salary, signing bonus, incentives actually paid, transfer fee), or US$20,000 per remaining month for an in-season signing; +US$100,000 per year on re-signing. (Money details belong to the contracts research; listed here only because they define the category.)
- Not eligible for the Rookie of the Year award.
- Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11353 , https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11879 , https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11878 ; 외국인선수 고용규정 Articles 2, 3, 4, 8, 9 in BYLAW.

**Developmental players (육성선수)**
- Players signed outside the draft. They are not counted in the 68-player limit. Of each year's high-school graduates a club may register at most 5 (3 for a club that drafted 7 or fewer players the previous year).
- A developmental player may be converted to a registered player from 1 May each year (and must then fit in the 68).
- They may play in exhibition games and in the Futures League, not in first-team regular-season games.
- Sources: BYLAW Articles 116, 117, 143; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11823

**Futures League entry**: 26 players per game day (28 on doubleheader days). Source: REG Article 68; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11841

## d2. Military service (added 2026-11-17)

- **Sangmu (국군체육부대) intakes.** Two a year. KBO players accepted: 14 in the first intake of 2022, 15 in the first half of 2024 (joined in June), 15 in the first half of 2025 (entered the army training centre on 12 May 2025, to serve until November 2026), 11 in the second half of 2026 (to enter on 7 December 2026 and serve until June 2028). Selection: documents, then a fitness test. Sources: OSEN (2022 first intake, 14 players) https://osen.co.kr/article/G1111709813 ; Sports Kyunghyang, 28 March 2024 https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202403281700033 ; Nate Sports, 10 April 2025 https://sports.news.nate.com/view/20250410n40015 ; Daum (2026 second half, 11 players) https://v.daum.net/v/6TtRikEoyu ; Xports News https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1985009
- **Length.** About 18 months, from the dates above (May 2025 to November 2026; December 2026 to June 2028). Not checked against the Military Service Act's text.
- **Who may apply to Sangmu.** Active-duty enlistees, 27 or younger on 31 December of the year, physical grade 3 or better (some grade 4). KBO players apply on the KBO's recommendation. The same article reports the decision to disband the police baseball team, the other team for serving players. Source: Hankook Ilbo, 17 October 2018 https://www.hankookilbo.com/news/article/201810171678773483 (the most recent official wording found; a later fan Q&A says 28, unconfirmed).
- **Exemption (체육요원).** Olympic medal (top three) or Asian Games gold: four weeks of basic training and 544 hours of sports volunteer work over 34 months, playing on meanwhile. Sources: Newsis, 20 November 2019 https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20191120_0000836175 ; Kyunghyang, 6 October 2026 (the defence ministry plans to cut or end supplementary service, not yet law) https://khan.co.kr/article/202610061519001
- **Asian Games results.** Korea won the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya final against Japan 3-1 (27 September 2026), its fifth gold in a row from 2010 and seventh in all. Sources: Weekly Kyunghyang https://weekly.khan.co.kr/article/202609272258001 ; Money Today https://www.mt.co.kr/world/2026/09/27/2026092722065426708 . Baseball has been at nine Asian Games since 1994 (1998, 2002, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022, 2026 gold; the 1994 and 2006 results not checked here).
- **Asian Games team (2026).** 24 players, 11 pitchers and 13 position players; 25 or younger (or within the fourth professional season) bar three wildcards (Kwak Bin, Noh Si-hwan, Moon Bo-gyeong); at most three per club, as the Games fell in the season. Sources: Newsis, 11 June 2026 https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20260611_0003665551 ; OSEN https://osen.co.kr/article/G1112120948
- **Rosters.** Players in military service do not count against the 68-player limit or the 63-player reserve list (BYLAW Articles 24 and 57; section d).

## e. Playing rules in force in 2026 that a simulation should model

**Automated ball-strike system (ABS)** - all regular pitches, from the exhibition season; the plate umpire relays the call; no appeal.
- Top of zone: 55.75% of the batter's height. Bottom: 27.04% of the batter's height (measured from the ground). These values date from 2025 (lowered 0.6 percentage points from 56.35% / 27.64% in 2024); there was NO zone change for 2026. Sources: PAGE (https://www.koreabaseball.com/Kbo/League/GameManage2026.aspx); https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=10321
- Width: home plate (43.18 cm) plus 2 cm on each side = 47.18 cm, judged once at the middle plane of the plate. Source: PAGE.
- Height is judged twice: the pitch must satisfy the top and bottom limits at both the middle plane and the rear (end) plane of the plate. Source: PAGE.
- The rear-plane zone is set 1.5 cm lower than the middle-plane zone to allow for the ball's drop. Source: KBO explanation reported by Nocut News, 26 April 2024, https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/6136307 ; the KBO stated in December 2024 that the middle/rear planes and the width were left unchanged (https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=10321).
- The zone does not depend on batting stance or on where the catcher receives the ball; each player's height is measured by the league/clubs. Source: https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1321510/
- NOT CONFIRMED: whether any part of the ball touching the zone is enough, and whether height is measured barefoot.
- If the system fails, the plate umpire calls balls and strikes; maximum stoppage to repair 5 minutes. Source: REG "ABS 규정".
- The KBO web page image still shows the 2024 percentages; the page text has the current ones.

**Pitch clock (2026 values)** - Sources: REG "KBO 피치클락 규정"; PAGE; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11795
- Between pitches: 18 seconds with bases empty, 23 seconds with runners on (2025: 20 / 25; cut by 2 seconds for 2026).
- Between batters: 33 seconds. Inning break: 2 minutes 10 seconds. Pitching change: 2 minutes 10 seconds. Mound visit: 30 seconds.
- Catcher must be in the catcher's box with 9 seconds left; batter must be in the box and ready with 8 seconds left; the pitcher must start his delivery before 0.
- Penalties: pitcher or catcher violation = automatic ball; batter violation = automatic strike; the ball is dead.
- Batter time-outs: 2 per plate appearance; a 3rd request followed by leaving the box = strike.
- The clock does not apply to the first pitch of the game. After an inning break or pitching change the first pitch may not be started with more than 20 seconds left (broadcast protection). The last warm-up pitch must start with at least 33 seconds left.
- Pickoffs / disengagements: NO LIMIT in the first-team league. A pickoff throw, fake or step-off with a runner on stops the clock and it restarts at 23 seconds.
- Futures League differences: 30 seconds between batters; 1 batter time-out; disengagement limit of 3 per plate appearance (reset when a runner advances); a 4th disengagement is a balk unless it produces an out or a runner advances.

**Base size**: 18 inches (45.72 cm) square, instead of 15 inches. Source: RULES 2.03 (https://6ptotvmi5753.edge.naverncp.com/KBO_FILE/ebook/pdf/2026_야구규칙.pdf)

**Shift restriction** (RULES 5.02(c), KBO text)
- When the pitcher starts his delivery, at least 4 fielders (not counting pitcher and catcher) must have both feet inside the outer edge of the infield dirt.
- When the pitch is released, at least 2 of them must be completely on each side of second base.
- The two infielders on each side may not switch sides during an inning (except with a substitution).
- Penalty, strengthened for 2026: if the infielder who violated is the first to touch the batted ball, the batter gets first base and every runner advances one base (error charged to the fielder, no at-bat), or the offense may take the result of the play. In other cases: automatic ball, or the offense takes the play. Shift violations are reviewable at any time.
- Sources: RULES 5.02(c); https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11790

**Designated hitter**: used in all KBO and Futures games (RULES 5.11 as adopted by the league; the starting DH must bat at least once against the opposing starter unless that starter has been replaced). Source: RULES 5.11; REG Articles 15 and 76.

**Extra innings**: regular season up to 11, then a tie; postseason up to 15; doubleheader games 9 only; no tie-break runner in the first-team league (Futures: tie-break in innings 10-12). Sources: REG Articles 1, 5, 59.

**Video review** (REG Article 28)
- 2 challenges per club in regulation innings; if both are upheld (calls overturned) the club gets 1 more; 1 more is added in extra innings. Home-run calls and shift violations can be reviewed at any time without using a challenge.
- The manager must ask within 30 seconds of the call (within 10 seconds for the last out of an inning or game). The review centre has 3 minutes; if no clear evidence is found the call stands.
- New in 2026: review of the "strategic overrun" at second/third base on force plays; a clear error found on a different play during a review can also be corrected; first- and second-base umpires wear a wireless intercom. Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11814 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11878

**Check-swing review** (in the first-team league since 19 August 2025; all of 2026)
- It is a swing if the angle of the bat head passes 90 degrees, i.e. goes beyond the line parallel to the front edge of home plate toward the pitcher; judged by the bat head only.
- 2 challenges per club, separate from ordinary challenges; a successful challenge is not used up; 1 more in extra innings.
- The offense can challenge only a "swing" call, the defense only a "no swing" call. Bunt attempts cannot be reviewed.
- Not used in the Futures League in 2026.
- Sources: REG Article 28.3(15) and 28.5; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11613 ; https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11841

**Three-batter minimum**: NOT in the first-team league (a relief pitcher must only finish the batter he comes in against, or the inning). It applies in the Futures League only: starter and relievers must face at least three consecutive batters or finish the inning, with injury exceptions. Source: RULES 5.10(g).

**Mound visits** (REG "경기의 스피드업")
- Manager/coach: 2 visits per game not counting pitching changes; a further visit forces a pitching change. A second visit to the same pitcher means he must be replaced. Visits for an injury or a pitcher hit by a batted ball do not count.
- Catcher: 2 visits per game in regulation, 1 more in extra innings; exceeding it forces the catcher's replacement.
- Each visit is limited to 30 seconds. Other infielders may not gather at the mound except during such a visit.
- The offense may call time for tactics only once per inning.

**Other KBO-specific items**
- Intentional walk without pitches, on the manager's signal. Source: REG "경기의 스피드업" 4.
- A fastball that hits or grazes the batter's head means automatic ejection of the pitcher, intent or not; a head-high fastball that misses draws a warning. Source: same.
- A pitcher may ask for a new ball at most 3 times per inning. Source: same.
- From 2026 obstruction on a pickoff attempt gives the runner one base (previously he was returned to the base). Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11790
- From 2026 regular foreign-substance checks: starters at least twice per game, relievers at least once; a violation is an ejection and a 10-game suspension. Source: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=11878
- Runner's lane: since 2025 the batter-runner may also run on the dirt inside the first-base line. Source: PAGE.
- Rain: regular-season games can be called (shortened games and "no game" apply under the official rules); the postseason uses suspension only. Sources: REG Articles 20, 46.
- Heat rules added in August 2026: a game is cancelled if the top-level heat alert (폭염중대경보) is in force for the park's area by 13:00; with lower alerts the start may be delayed to 19:30 at the latest or the game cancelled when the on-site apparent temperature reaches 35 C; 4-minute cooling breaks after the 3rd and 7th innings (and after the 9th in extra innings). Sources: https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12097 and https://www.koreabaseball.com/MediaNews/Notice/View.aspx?bdSe=12108
